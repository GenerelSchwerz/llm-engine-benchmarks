A service accepts requests, puts them in a bounded queue, and sends each request to one of several workers. Workers can take different amounts of time and may fail after accepting a request.

Explain a design that keeps the service responsive under bursty load. Cover queue capacity, backpressure, timeouts, retries, duplicate work, and how a caller learns the final outcome. Give one concrete example with three requests arriving while all workers are busy. Finish with two failure cases you would test and the measurements you would collect to distinguish queueing delay from processing time.
